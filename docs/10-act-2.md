# 10 — Act 2: three maps past the tower

Decided with the user on 2026-10-02 and built the same day (P3, A2-01…05). This is what was asked
for, what was built, and what is still a stand-in.

## 1. What was asked

- **Monsters of the families act 1 already has bonuses against** (animal, orc, undead, devil,
  mystic) — only the theme changes: "orchi di fuoco, animali di pietra". Every line, stone and
  bonus the player already owns keeps its worth.
- **One or two new pieces of gear a map, none of them an armour or a weapon.** The tower's stay
  until the next dungeon.
- **Every new piece upgrades with parts dropped in the new maps.**
- **Creatures strong against a character in the tower's gear, upgraded and with its bonuses.** The
  measure is the user's own level-28 Warrior, kept as `src/Kiln.Tools/Fixtures/warrior-28.sav`.
- Tuned on **half** the right family bonus: the right stones help, and are not required.

## 2. The maps

| # | Map | Levels | Families | Creatures | Boss | Shard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Selva di Pietra** (the Stonewood) | 27–31 | animal, mystic | shard spider, slate wolf, basalt adder · moss golem, lichen cap, geode goleling | the Stone Stag | Petrified Heart |
| 2 | **Palude Sommersa** (the Drowned Mire) | 31–35 | undead, animal | mire crawler, the drowned, marsh brute, fen witch · bog toad, bog spitter, bog bloater | the Drowned King | Drowned Bell |

The way in is a new gate in the **north-west corner of the Broken Gate**, open at level 24 once the
tower is cleared. Each map is entered at a corner by a camp (safe ground, a shrine), crossed by a road
to the far corner, and has three shrines; the boss stands in a ring of its own behind a guard. The
quests are act 1's shape: a hunt (15), a shard, the boss — the boss opens the road on.

Roles are act 1's, role for role (bruiser, archer, shielder, mender, bomber, and a swarm), so the
shards' waves and the AI need nothing new.

## 4. How the numbers were set

`kiln balance --save src/Kiln.Tools/Fixtures/warrior-28.sav [--levels n] [--family 0.5] [--only text]`
builds the saved character as the game does — points, skills and ranks, every worn piece with its
upgrade, lines and stones — and fights it against every creature: one alone, three packs of eight,
and the bosses. Its best family bonus is counted against everything by `--family` (half, here).

The reference: attack 463, health 1747, defence 429, +39% blows, +54% skills. It clears the tower's
last floor in seconds (the Demon Lord in 37), so act 2 starts well above it:

| | A pack creature | Three packs of eight | A boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health | 9,000–12,000 (the tower's: 1,700) | about 30–40 s, 2 to 2.5 health bars taken | 410,000–430,000: 95–125 s, 2 to 3.3 bars |
| A blow | about 5% of the Warrior's health | | |

Maps two and three were checked with `--levels 4` and `--levels 8`. A level adds little next to
gear, so the later maps rise gently. This is a fight of averages with nothing dodged: it says the
maps are hard for that character, not exactly how hard. **The user's playing is the real test.**

## 5. How the maps are made

- **Scenes** are written by `kiln scenes` from `src/Kiln.Tools/Commands/Act2Layouts.cs` (act 1's
  were written by a script that was never kept). Change the layout, run the command;
  `verify.sh` fails if a scene is older than its layout.
- **Map pieces**: act 1's models painted over in one colour (`"recolour"` and `"glow"` on a kit
  piece) — stone trees and stone deer, black rock, crystal and lava that glow.
- **Creatures**: act 1's models turned toward one hue (`"visual_recolour"`, `"visual_glow"` on an
  enemy), each colour keeping how light or dark it was. Before this a creature's `visual_tint`
  did nothing to a model.
- **Ours, made in Blender**: the three shard stones, the five headpieces, three beasts and what the two-legged
  bosses wear (`art/blender/models`, §6), and the icons of all the new gear and parts.

## 6. What was a stand-in, and is no longer (2026-10-02, at your call)

- **The beasts are ours.** The slate wolf, the ember hound and the Stone Stag are made with the creature kit
  (`models/slate_wolf.py`, `ember_hound.py`, `stone_stag.py`): slabs of slate and crystal through the hide, fire
  along the spine, antlers of crystal.
- **The two-legged bosses wear pieces of ours.** A visual can hang models on a creature's bones
  (`"attachments"`: bone, model, size and place as shares of the bone's height). The Drowned King has a crown
  of green bronze and coral and a mantle of weed; the Burning Warlord a horned helm with a crest of flame and
  spiked shoulders (`art/blender/bosskit.py`). Their bodies are still act 1's, recoloured.
- **Each boss gives a look** (REF-23): the Stone Fawn (a companion), the Marshlight Aura, and from the Warlord
  the Cinder Aura and the Ember Cub. A companion can be recoloured as a creature is.
- **The parts have rendered icons**, built in `trinkets.py` like the gear's. The two new stones keep the drawn
  icon every stone has.
- **An outpost and two pedlars.** A smith (Marek) and a merchant (Sella) at the Stonewood's way in, so the new
  gear is upgraded where its parts drop; a pedlar at the Mire's camp (Oswin) and at the Cinderlands' (Ketta).
- **The engine message is gone.** "material is null" on entering the Mire and the Cinderlands came from the
  recoloured pools (water, lava): a whole-mesh override laid over the pool scene's own surface overrides. A
  recolour now replaces each surface instead.
- **Experience is checked.** `kiln simulate` walks act 2 from level 26 — the tower's creatures stop paying five
  levels over them, so a player arrives between 24 and 28 — entering the maps at 26, 30 and 35 and ending at
  39–40.

## 7. Still open

- The other creatures (spiders, adders, golems, the drowned, orcs) are act 1's models
  recoloured (six now have models of their own, §8): the same kind in another land's colours, which is what was asked. Models of our own for the
  two-legged ones need a humanoid rig we do not have yet.
- **Gold is estimated, not simulated.** Taking a new piece to +9 costs about 0.6–0.9 million yang in fees before
  failures (roughly twice that with them), some 150 common parts and 15–20 rare ones: several hundred kills and
  a dozen bosses or shards a piece. That is a long grind on purpose; say if it should be shorter.
